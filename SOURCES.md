# Desk Prep — public demo

A static web app to prepare Sales & Trading / Global Markets interviews: 1883 flashcards (options, rates/FX/credit,
markets, structuring, quant, corporate finance, brainteasers, fit and CV questions), spaced repetition, a daily plan,
puzzles with hints and a mental-maths drill. Built by Pierre Fourel for his own preparation.

Demo build: no account, no server-side storage. Your progress is kept only in your browser (localStorage);
clearing site data resets it. The AI mock interview of the private version is disabled.

## Run locally

    python3 -m http.server 8000   # from the repository root
    # then open http://localhost:8000/fr/ or http://localhost:8000/en/

`cards.json` and `deep.json` (the « Comprendre » texts, loaded after the first render) are fetched with `fetch()`, so opening `index.html` directly from disk (file://) will not work.
